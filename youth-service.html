Free counselling for young people, right here in our community.

MindZone is launching a free partnership community counselling pilot for young people aged 13–18 in Haldane and the surrounding areas — a safe, confidential space to talk, get support, and look after their mental health and wellbeing.

This page is for registering interest in the upcoming service — it isn't for urgent support. If a young person is in crisis or at risk right now, please contact Childline free on 0800 1111, Samaritans free on 116 123, or call 999.

What's on offer

  • Free, easy-to-access counselling sessions for young people (assessment-based — a waiting list may apply if demand is high)
  • A friendly, trusted, supportive environment to talk, heal, grow and thrive
  • Professional support for emotional, mental health and wellbeing needs

If interest grows, MindZone aims to build on this project through fundraising, volunteering, and further community partnerships — so no local young person has to struggle alone while waiting on ever-increasing NHS lists.
